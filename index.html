    PROVENANCE AND SAFETY

    A deliberately narrow public boundary

    Included

    Eight fictional device records, nine fictional price observations, twelve fictional methods records, and generated same-origin source pages.

    Excluded

    Production data, member records, user content, accounts, analytics, cookies, persistence, arbitrary network requests, product images, and third-party product marks.

    Interpretation

    Results are demonstrations of structured evidence handling—not specifications, offers, publications, purchasing recommendations, or proof of laboratory use.
